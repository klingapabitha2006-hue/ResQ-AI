About ResQ AI

Intelligent Disaster Response

ResQ AI combines disaster information, location intelligence and artificial intelligence to support faster emergency response decisions.

Why ResQ AI?

During disasters, rescue teams may receive multiple emergency reports at the same time. Identifying the most critical incident quickly can be difficult.

ResQ AI analyses important emergency factors and generates a Rescue Priority Score so that critical incidents can be identified and handled efficiently.

What ResQ AI Analyses

🌊 Disaster Severity
👥 Number of Affected People
📍 Incident Location
🛣️ Road Accessibility
🏥 Nearby Hospital Availability
🌦️ Weather Conditions

How It Works

From Emergency Report to Rescue Decision

ResQ AI follows a simple process to help emergency teams understand and prioritize incidents.

1

Report

An emergency incident is reported with important information such as disaster type, location and affected people.

2

Analyse

ResQ AI analyses disaster, location, people, road, hospital and weather information.

3

Prioritize

The system generates a Rescue Priority Score and assigns an appropriate priority level.

4

Rescue Decision

Rescue teams can use the information to identify critical incidents and plan their response.
